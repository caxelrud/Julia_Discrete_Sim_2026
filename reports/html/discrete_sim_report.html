Experiments

Replications, a detected warmup, batch means for a single run, paired comparisons and a factorial design -- all with common random numbers.

How long the transient lasts

Welch's procedure on the wip series over 4 replications suggests discarding the first 3830 time units (plateau 13.3 ± 1.912). That is most of the horizon: for this series the diagnostic could not find a plateau -- the curve moves by 1.912 around 13.3 all the way to the end -- so the number below is a design decision, not a measurement: more replications or a longer window would settle it.
